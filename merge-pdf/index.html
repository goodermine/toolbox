    FAQ

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. The whole merge happens on your device with the open-source pdf-lib library — nothing is sent to a server. That matters for large or sensitive documents (contracts, scans, IDs).

    Will it reduce quality or DPI?

    No. Pages are copied as-is, so a 300 DPI scan stays 300 DPI. The merged file is roughly the combined size of the parts — nothing is re-compressed.

    How do I get the order right?

    Add the files, then use the ↑ / ↓ buttons (or drag) to arrange them top-to-bottom. The merged PDF follows that order — e.g. Part 1 above Part 2.

    Is there a size limit?

    No fixed limit, but everything runs in your browser's memory, so very large files (hundreds of MB) depend on your device. Two-part 300 DPI documents are well within range.
